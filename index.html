Good to know

Frequently asked.

Need a detail that is not covered here? Send us an inquiry and we’ll help.

How much does each bike cost?

ENGWE EP-2 Pro costs €140 per month, VITILAN V3 costs €140 per month, and ChamRider DTS425 costs €150 per month. Confirm the rental period and availability before booking.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. The listed deposit is €50 for each model. Confirm the final rental conditions with E-Wheels.

What is included with the rental?

A mobile holder and chain lock are included. Flat-tire repair is free for up to two times, and brake-pad replacement is included free.

Do I need a driving licence?

Yes, a valid driving licence is required for the rental.

What is the minimum rental duration?

E-Wheels rentals are available for one month or longer. Select your intended monthly duration in the inquiry form and contact us to confirm availability and final terms.

How do I check availability?

Use the inquiry form below. It will prepare a WhatsApp message with your selected bike and contact details.
